# iPet Lovers - Pet Image Gallery & Marketplace

The #1 web app for pet lovers to discover, share, and trade pet images from around the world.

## Features

### Main Website
- **Modern Responsive Design** - Works perfectly on all devices
- **Pet Gallery** - Browse thousands of free pet images
- **Categories** - Dogs, Cats, Birds, Fish, Rabbits, and more
- **Marketplace** - Buy and sell premium pet images
- **Upload System** - Easy image upload with categorization
- **Filter Options** - Filter by free, premium, or for sale
- **User Authentication** - Login and register system
- **Buy/Download** - Purchase premium images or download free ones

### User Profile System
- **Personal Dashboard** - View your stats and activity
- **My Listings** - Manage your uploaded pet images
- **Purchase History** - Track all your purchases
- **Sales History** - Monitor your sales and earnings
- **Account Settings** - Customize your profile and preferences
- **Seller/Buyer Modes** - Choose to sell, buy, or both
- **Earnings Tracking** - See your total earnings from sales

### Admin Dashboard
- **Complete Control Panel** - Manage all aspects of the platform
- **Dashboard Analytics** - View stats, revenue, and user activity
- **Image Management** - Approve, edit, or delete images
- **User Management** - Monitor and manage users
- **Sales Overview** - Track all transactions
- **Category Statistics** - Visual distribution of pet types

## Technologies Used
- HTML5
- CSS3 (Modern responsive design)
- JavaScript (Vanilla JS)
- Font Awesome Icons
- Unsplash API for sample images

## Getting Started

1. Open `index.html` in your browser to view the main website
2. Click "Login" to create an account or login
3. Browse the gallery and marketplace
4. Click "Buy Now" on premium images or "Download Free" on free images
5. Upload your own pet images to sell
6. Visit `profile.html` to manage your listings and view your activity
7. Open `admin.html` to access the admin dashboard (admin only)

## How It Works

### For Buyers
1. Register/Login to your account
2. Browse the gallery or marketplace
3. Click "Buy Now" on images you like
4. View your purchases in your profile

### For Sellers
1. Register/Login and enable "Seller" mode
2. Click "Upload" to add your pet images
3. Set pricing (Free, Premium, or For Sale)
4. Track your sales and earnings in your profile
5. Manage your listings (edit/delete)

### For Admins
1. Access the admin dashboard at `admin.html`
2. View analytics and statistics
3. Manage all images, users, and sales
4. Monitor platform activity
