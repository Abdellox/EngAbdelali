# 🎯 Job Interview Prep

A free, interactive website to help people prepare for job interviews across 22+ career categories and 100+ job roles.

## 🌟 Features

- **22 Career Categories** covering everything from IT to Healthcare to Skilled Trades
- **100+ Job Roles** with specific interview questions
- **300+ Interview Questions** with detailed answers
- **🌍 Multi-Language Support** - Translate to 50+ languages including Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Russian, and many more!
- **Interactive Interface** with smooth navigation
- **Search Functionality** to quickly find your job role
- **Mobile Responsive** design works on all devices
- **100% Free** - A gift to help people succeed

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. **Select your language** using the Google Translate dropdown in the header
3. Browse categories or use the search bar to find your job role
4. Click on any job to see interview questions
5. Navigate through questions and reveal answers
6. Practice and prepare for your interview!

## 🌍 Supported Languages
