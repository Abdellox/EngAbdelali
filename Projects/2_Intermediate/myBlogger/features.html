🚀 Powerful Features

Everything you need to create an amazing blog experience

Reading Progress Bar

Visual indicator showing how much of the article readers have completed. Keeps users engaged and informed about their reading progress.

NEW

Bookmark & Save

Let readers save articles for later reading. Bookmarks are stored locally and persist across sessions.

POPULAR

Reactions & Claps

Engage with content through reactions. Readers can show appreciation with unlimited claps, similar to Medium.

INTERACTIVE

Auto Table of Contents

Automatically generated navigation from article headings. Makes long articles easy to navigate and scan.

SMART

Code Syntax Highlighting

Beautiful code blocks with one-click copy functionality. Perfect for technical blogs and tutorials.

DEV-FRIENDLY

Social Sharing

Share articles on Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, Reddit, and more. Includes native share API support for mobile.

VIRAL

Dark Mode

Eye-friendly dark theme with smooth transitions. Preference is saved and persists across visits.

ESSENTIAL

Fully Responsive

Perfect experience on all devices - desktop, tablet, and mobile. Mobile-first design approach.

MOBILE-READY

Follow Authors

Build your audience by letting readers follow their favorite authors. Track follower counts and engagement.

COMMUNITY

Series & Collections

Organize related articles into series. Perfect for multi-part tutorials and themed content.

ORGANIZED

View Counter

Track article views and display popularity metrics. Helps identify trending content.

ANALYTICS

Reading Time

Automatically calculated reading time based on word count. Helps readers plan their time.

HELPFUL

Smart Search

Fast and intuitive search functionality. Find articles quickly with keyword matching.

Lazy Loading

Images load as you scroll, improving initial page load time and performance.

PERFORMANCE

Scroll to Top

Quick navigation back to the top of long articles. Appears automatically when scrolling down.

Newsletter Integration

Built-in newsletter signup forms. Ready to integrate with Mailchimp, ConvertKit, and more.

Strategic ad placement zones ready for Google AdSense and other ad networks.
