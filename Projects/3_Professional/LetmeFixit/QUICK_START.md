# Quick Start Guide - FixIt Website

## 🚀 Getting Started (30 seconds)

1. **Open the website:**
   - Double-click `index.html` 
   - OR right-click → Open with → Your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)

2. **Search for your problem:**
   - Type in the search box (e.g., "HP laptop won't turn on")
   - See instant suggestions as you type
   - Click a suggestion or press Enter

3. **View the repair guide:**
   - Click on any guide card
   - Read through step-by-step instructions
   - Follow each step carefully

That's it! You're ready to fix anything.

## 📖 How to Use a Repair Guide

### When you open a guide, you'll see:

1. **Overview Section**
   - What the problem is
   - Tools you'll need
   - Parts required (if any)

2. **Step-by-Step Instructions**
   - Each step is numbered
   - Detailed substeps to follow
   - ⚠️ **Warnings** in yellow boxes (read these!)
   - 💡 **Tips** in green boxes (helpful tricks)

3. **Additional Information**
   - Common causes of the problem
   - Prevention tips for the future

### Pro Tips:
- ✅ Read ALL steps before starting
- ✅ Gather all tools first
- ✅ Take photos before disassembly
- ✅ Work in good lighting
- ✅ Don't rush - follow each substep
- ✅ Print the guide if needed (Print button at bottom)

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

### Before Starting Any Repair:

1. **Backup your data** (if applicable)
2. **Charge your device** (if it's a phone/laptop)
3. **Read the entire guide first**
4. **Gather all tools**
5. **Work in a clean, well-lit area**
6. **Take your time**

### Safety First:

- ⚠️ Always unplug devices before opening
- ⚠️ Ground yourself to prevent static damage
- ⚠️ Don't force anything
- ⚠️ If unsure, stop and seek professional help
- ⚠️ Keep track of screws and small parts

## ❓ Troubleshooting the Website

### Search not working?
- Make sure `repair-database.js` is in the same folder
- Refresh the page (F5 or Ctrl+R)
- Check browser console for errors (F12)

### Guides not showing?
- Ensure all 3 files are together: `index.html`, `script.js`, `repair-database.js`
- Try a different browser
- Clear browser cache

### Modal won't close?
- Click the X button in top right
- Click outside the modal
- Press Escape key
- Refresh the page

## 🌟 Success Tips

### For Software Fixes:
1. Try the easiest solution first
2. Restart between major changes
3. Backup before resetting
4. Update software when prompted
5. One change at a time

### For Hardware Fixes:
1. Take photos during disassembly
2. Organize screws by step
3. Don't force connections
4. Ground yourself first
5. Work slowly and carefully

## 📞 When to Seek Professional Help

Stop and get professional help if:

- ❌ You're uncomfortable with any step
- ❌ Something doesn't match the guide
- ❌ You hear unusual sounds
- ❌ You smell burning
- ❌ Device is under warranty (don't void it!)
- ❌ Repair involves high voltage
- ❌ You don't have the right tools
