# Enhanced Features

## What's New ✨

### 1. **PDF Preview in All Tools**
- When you upload a PDF, you can now SEE all pages before making changes
- Visual grid layout showing all pages as thumbnails
- Page numbers displayed clearly

### 2. **Visual Page Selection**
- **Split PDF**: Click on pages you want to extract (checkboxes appear)
- **Remove Pages**: Click on pages you want to delete
- Selected pages are highlighted in blue
- Much easier than typing page numbers!

### 3. **New Edit PDF Tool** ✏️
- Open and view your entire PDF
- Add text annotations
- Draw on pages
- Add images
- Highlight sections
- Full visual editor toolbar

### 4. **Better User Experience**
- See what you're working with before making changes
- No more guessing which pages to select
- Visual feedback for all operations
- Preview before download

## How It Works

1. **Choose a tool** (e.g., "Split PDF" or "Remove Pages")
2. **Upload your PDF** - All pages appear as thumbnails
3. **Click on pages** to select them (they turn blue)
4. **Click the action button** to process
5. **Download** your modified PDF

## Example Workflows

### Split PDF
1. Click "Split PDF"
2. Upload your document
3. See all 10 pages displayed
4. Click pages 3, 5, and 7
5. Click "Extract Selected Pages"
6. Download your new 3-page PDF

### Remove Pages
1. Click "Remove Pages"
2. Upload your document
3. See all pages
4. Click unwanted pages (they highlight)
5. Click "Remove Selected Pages"
6. Download cleaned PDF

### Edit PDF
1. Click "Edit PDF"
2. Upload your document
3. Use toolbar to add text, draw, or add images
4. Click "Save PDF"
5. Download edited version
