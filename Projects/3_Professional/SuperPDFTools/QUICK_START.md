# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - PDF Editor

## **Get Started in 30 Seconds!**

### **Step 1: Open the Editor** (5 seconds)
1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Click the big **"Edit PDF"** card (the one with the gold star ⭐)

### **Step 2: Upload Your PDF** (5 seconds)
1. Click the upload area OR drag your PDF file
2. Wait 2 seconds for it to load

### **Step 3: Edit Text** (10 seconds)
1. **Hover** over any text → It highlights in red
2. **Click** the text → Edit popup appears
3. **Type** your changes → See live preview
4. **Click "Save Changes"** → Text updates instantly!

### **Step 4: Save Your PDF** (10 seconds)
1. Click **"💾 Save Edited PDF"** button
2. Wait a few seconds
3. Download your edited PDF!

---

## 🎨 **What You Can Do**

### **Edit Text**
- Click any word like "Kiro"
- Change it to "Kimo"
- Updates instantly!

### **Change Colors**
- Click text
- Pick a color (red, blue, green, etc.)
- Text changes color immediately!

### **Change Size**
- Click text
- Change size (8-72)
- Text gets bigger/smaller instantly!

### **Delete Text**
- Click text
- Click "Delete" button
- Text disappears instantly!

## 🆘 **Troubleshooting**

**Q: Text doesn't highlight when I hover?**
A: Make sure you're hovering directly over the text. Try moving your mouse slowly.

**Q: Changes don't appear?**
A: Make sure you clicked "Save Changes" in the popup, not just closed it.

**Q: Can't find the text I want to edit?**
A: Use the Previous/Next buttons to navigate through pages.

**Q: PDF won't upload?**
A: Make sure it's a valid PDF file (not an image or other format).
