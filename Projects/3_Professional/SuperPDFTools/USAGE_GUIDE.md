# 📖 PDF Editor - Complete Usage Guide

## 🌟 Main Feature: Edit PDF

The **Edit PDF** tool allows you to modify existing PDFs like a professional editor!

### ✏️ Edit Existing Text (NEW!)

**Click on ANY text in the PDF to:**
- ✍️ **Edit the text** - Change "I love you Kiro" to "I love you Kimo"
- 🎨 **Change color** - Make individual words red, blue, green, etc.
- 📏 **Change font size** - Make text bigger or smaller
- 🗑️ **Delete text** - Remove unwanted words or sentences

**Example:**
1. Upload your PDF
2. Click on the word "Kiro" in the text "I love you Kiro"
3. A popup appears with:
   - Text editor (change "Kiro" to "Kimo")
   - Font size slider
   - Color picker (change to red, blue, etc.)
   - Delete button
4. Click "Save" to apply changes
5. The word is now changed!

### ➕ Add New Content

**Click toolbar buttons, then click on PDF:**

1. **Add Text** ✏️
   - Click "Add Text" button
   - Click anywhere on PDF
   - Type your text
   - Drag to reposition

2. **Add Image** 🖼️
   - Click "Add Image" button
   - Click on PDF where you want it
   - Select image file
   - Drag to move, resize as needed

3. **Add Signature** ✍️
   - Click "Add Signature" button
   - Click on PDF
   - Type your signature
   - Appears in italic bold style

4. **Add Link** 🔗
   - Click "Add Link" button
   - Click on PDF
   - Enter URL and link text
   - Creates clickable blue link

### 📱 Responsive Design

- **Desktop**: Full sidebar with all tools
- **Tablet**: Sidebar moves to top
- **Mobile**: Optimized touch interface
- **Auto-scaling**: PDF fits your screen perfectly

### 🎯 Step-by-Step Example

**Scenario: Edit a contract**

1. **Upload PDF**
   - Click "Edit PDF" card
   - Upload your contract.pdf

2. **Edit existing text**
   - Click on "John Doe" → Change to "Jane Smith"
   - Click on "$1000" → Change to "$2000"
   - Click on date → Update to new date

3. **Add signature**
   - Click "Add Signature" button
   - Click at bottom of contract
   - Type your name
   - Drag to position perfectly

4. **Add date**
   - Click "Add Text" button
   - Click next to signature
   - Type today's date

5. **Save**
   - Click "Save Edited PDF"
   - Download your edited contract!
